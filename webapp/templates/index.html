HOW TO INTERPRET THIS DASHBOARD

Good decisions start with good definitions.

Counts describe published Cleveland CAD records, not independently confirmed unique emergencies. Neighborhood-level totals use source-provided geographic labels matched to official boundary names; unmatched records are excluded. Transport/dispatch indicators are source flags, not measured response times.
