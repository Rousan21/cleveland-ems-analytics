FROM CHARTS TO DECISIONS

What can you do with these findings?

Use the dashboard to prioritize questions and data checks—not to make unsupported operational decisions.

03 — PRACTICAL USE
01

Investigate timing patterns

Find hours and weekdays with more published dispatch activity, then compare multiple historical periods before forming a hypothesis about demand.

Useful for:Choosing which time periods deserve closer analysis.
02

Check whether trends are reliable

Use the reporting-history monitor to determine whether older dates gain or lose records after daily imports.

Useful for:Deciding which apparent trends need a data-quality warning.
03

Identify gaps before analysis

Compare valid timestamp-pair coverage and distinguish verified neighborhood assignments from a reference-only map.

Useful for:Choosing defensible metrics and deciding what additional data is required.
What this does not establish

No verified unique incident counts, geographic hotspots, actual ambulance availability, staffing requirements, or official response-time performance metrics.
