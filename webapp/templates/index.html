HOW TO INTERPRET THIS DASHBOARD

Good decisions start with good definitions.

Counts describe published Cleveland CAD rows, not independently confirmed unique emergencies. Incident-type labels describe the dispatcher's classification, not a confirmed medical diagnosis. Neighborhood labels are matched to official boundaries. The City defines isTransport=true as an incident resulting in transporting a patient, and isDispatch=true as one or more units dispatched. These are row-level indicators, not independently verified numbers of unique patients, hospital admissions, dispatch units, or response times.
